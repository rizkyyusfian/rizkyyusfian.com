# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers, developers, peers, collaborators, and recruiters visiting Rizky's personal portfolio and digital garden. They come to understand his work, read his writing, browse projects and collections, or find a direct way to contact him.

## Product Purpose

Present Rizky's technical work and personal interests in one bilingual digital space. Success means visitors can quickly understand who he is, navigate writing and projects, and recognize a distinct personal voice without the interface getting in the way of reading.

## Brand Personality

Warm, technical, and personal. The interface should feel like a carefully maintained developer workspace with the comfort and readability of a personal library.

## Anti-references

- Fake desktop-window chrome or decorative operating-system controls.
- Generic SaaS dashboards and interchangeable portfolio templates.
- Loud gradients, bouncy motion, excessive glass effects, or animation that delays reading.
- Terminal styling used as costume instead of useful navigation and hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. Keep content central and immediately readable.
2. Let terminal and IDE language organize the experience without overpowering it.
3. Use motion to explain state changes and add personality, never to delay access.
4. Preserve a consistent bilingual experience across English and Indonesian routes.
5. Prefer lightweight native browser behavior and static Astro output where possible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Every interactive control needs a visible focus state and clear accessible name. Motion must honor `prefers-reduced-motion`, with all content available immediately when motion is reduced or scripts fail.
