Every December I promise myself I'll write one of these while it's still fresh,
and every year I write it in the last hour of the last day. So here it is —
2025, honestly, before I forget how it actually felt.

The short version: this was the year with two halves. Bandung and the student
chapter closing, then Sorong and a working life beginning. On one side, I saw
what I'd built. On the other, I started building something new.

## School

I **finished the thesis in February**. After all of 2024's grinding, the ending
was quieter than I expected — a last round of edits, a submission, and then that
strange emptiness when the thing that owned you for two years suddenly doesn't.
Later in the year, a part of it was **published in a Q2 journal**, which is the
proudest research line on my CV.

Then the slow dismantling of student life: **packing everything out** now that
S2 was done, selling some things, shipping the rest home to Sorong. And on
**10 May 2025, wisuda** — the master's, official. Second graduation, and it hit
different from the first. This one felt like the end of school for good.

## Work

On **3 June 2025 I received my SK CPNS** — the call I'd been waiting on since
2024 finally came. I **started work at BKDD Kabupaten Sorong**, and just like
that I went from student to civil servant.

The adjustment was the real story. New environment, new colleagues, and an
entirely different shape to the day — no more all-nighters, no more thesis room,
just a steady work-life rhythm I'm still learning to hold. **July through
October was mostly just work**, heads-down, settling in. In between I got my
first **dinas (work trip) to Manokwari** — first time in the city, and I made
sure to look around while I was there.

## Life

The big move: after years of the Bandung–Sorong shuttle, I **officially came
home to Sorong** for good. Before leaving Bandung there was a proper send-off —
a villa with my friends up in **Dago Utara**, because everyone was scattering to
different cities and we all knew it.

Family came to visit around graduation: we took a **3–4 day trip to Jakarta**,
then I brought them **around Bandung** — all the tourist spots, the cafés, the
food, playing tour guide in the city that had been mine. Back home, a **short
reunion with high-school friends** reminded me that some bonds just pick up
where they left off. Closed the year with a **new year at home, with family** —
which, after a year of goodbyes, was exactly right.

## Travel

- **Masjid Al Jabbar, Bandung** — the "floating mosque," and every bit as
  striking as the photos.
- **Camping at Batu Kuda, Gunung Manglayang** — one last mountain before leaving
  West Java.
- **Jakarta** with family, and the farewell **Bandung** tour.
- **Manokwari** — first visit, courtesy of work.

## Hobbies & games

Started **running** — and I'll be honest, I haven't really improved and I'm not
consistent about it. But I keep coming back to it, so that counts. Back in Sorong
I started a **weekly badminton** session with a high-school friend, which is
quietly the best routine I built all year. In November I **came back to
Valorant** after months away — a little washed, but happy to be back. And I
caught **_Marathon_ season two**. The
[Steam year in review](https://store.steampowered.com/replay/76561198142939511/2025)
has the rest.

## Gear

One upgrade that mattered: a new **iPhone 15**. After years on Android it's a
whole re-learning, but it's been a good companion for the new chapter.

## Carrying into 2026

- Build a real work-life rhythm — not just survive the adjustment, own it.
- Make running and badminton stick. Consistency over intensity.
- Keep one no-stakes project of my own alive at all times.
- Stay close to the people who scattered. Distance shouldn't mean silence.

2025 was the year the scaffolding came down and I got to see what I'd actually
built — then packed it up, went home, and started again. Not everything I hoped
for. More than I expected. On to the next.
