If 2023 was the fresh start, 2024 was the grind. Head down, thesis-first, for
most of the year — with just enough life around the edges to stay human. Here's
how it actually went.

## School

The thesis. Lord, the thesis. I'm not going to pretend it was smooth — I
struggled with it, a lot. By the second half of the year (semester four) it had
taken over completely: all day, all week, and more than a few nights sleeping
over in the thesis room because going home felt like a waste of momentum.

The wins that kept me going, though, were real:

- A **class project got written up** — published in a journal and presented at a
  conference.
- Part of my **thesis was published in a Sinta 2 journal** (UAD).

Seeing your name in print for the first time does something to you. It made the
grind feel like it was building toward something.

## Work & programs

Two facilitator roles this year, both around **Google Career Certificates — IT
Support**:

- **Google Career Certificates 2024 × Telkom** — I helped teach Telkom employees
  through the IT Support course (three months).
- **Digital Talent Scholarship × Google Career Certificates** — the same course I
  once took as a participant, except this time I was the facilitator.

Going from student to the person at the front of the room is a strange, good
feeling.

I also went back to **Sorong to apply for CPNS** — and got accepted with Pemda
Kabupaten Sorong. Now I'm just waiting on the call. No idea when it comes.

## Life

Between the thesis crunch and the trips home, the year had two speeds: buried, or
recharging. I'll admit one thing I'm not proud of — I **started vaping** this
year. Don't. That's the whole review on that.

## Travel

- **Jogja, first time** — a week over the semester break. Prambanan,
  Parangtritis, Malioboro, keliling the city by motorbike, and a stop at UGM. One
  of my favorite trips, full stop.
- **BEKRAF/Baparekraf Dev Day Bandung 2024** — and later an invite back for the
  evaluation session, which came with some merch (TWS earbuds and bits).
- **GDG Bandung 2024** — the DevFest habit continues.

## Hobbies & games

Picked up **badminton** this year — my new favorite way to move. Learned
**Golang** in my spare time just because it looked fun (it is). Still on
**Valorant**, still **Plat** — no shame, no progress, ha. And I started a new
show, **_Marathon_** — got through season one and I'm officially following it.
The [Steam year in review](https://store.steampowered.com/replay/76561198142939511/2024)
has the honest hours.

## Gear

A good year for the desk. I won a **tracer-study lottery from Ubaya** and walked
away with an **Oppo A58**. Then a proper refresh: **AJAZZ AK82 MAX HE** keyboard,
**IINNO X2V2** mouse, and an **MSI G255F** monitor. The setup finally feels like
mine.

## Carrying into 2025

- Finish. The. Thesis. No more excuses.
- Quit the vape. (Writing it here so I can't pretend I didn't.)
- Keep badminton and Golang alive through the crunch — they kept me sane.

2024 was the year I paid the price up front. Ended it the usual way, makan-makan
with friends on New Year's, tired but close to the finish line. 2025, let's
close this out.
