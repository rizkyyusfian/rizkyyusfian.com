2023 was a fresh-start year. New city, new degree, new field, new friends — most
of what was familiar in 2022 got swapped out. Somehow it felt more calm than
chaotic. Here's the year.

## School

I got accepted to **Telkom University** for my master's, and that decision
rearranged everything. My undergrad was software engineering; my S2 is data
science — so a big part of the year was un-learning old instincts and picking up
statistics, modeling, and a whole new way of thinking about problems.

The rhythm of grad school suited me. I made it through the final terms and tests,
took the semester break, and slowly stopped feeling like a tourist in my own
program.

## Work & programs

Two big ones outside of coursework:

- **GIGIH 3.0** (Generasi GIGIH) — applied, got in, and spent six months in the
  program. Graduation was held at the **Gojek office**, which felt like a proper
  milestone.
- **FGA Google IT Support** — a three-month course I applied to and finished.

Between the two, 2023 quietly became the year I collected the credentials and
habits that made the data-science switch feel real instead of theoretical.

## Life

The move itself was the story: **Surabaya to Bandung**. New apartment, new
routines, new everything. Bandung turned out to be an easy city to land in —
cooler weather, good coffee, an unhurried pace. I made a solid group of
**grad-school friends**, which makes any new city feel like home faster than
anything else.

For Lebaran I went back to **Sorong** for about ten days, then straight back to
Bandung for final-term tests. Later, another break home. The Bandung–Sorong
shuttle was becoming a fixture of my life.

## Travel

Mostly this was the year of exploring Bandung and around it:

- **Gedung Sate**, **Lapangan Gasibu**, **Kiara Artha Park**
- **Lembang** — the **Orchid Forest**
- The cafés up in **Dago Atas** (worth the drive for the view alone)
- **Kota Baru Parahyangan**
- A **villa in Lembang** with friends to celebrate surviving the semester

## Hobbies & games

Still on **Valorant** — climbed to **Plat 2**, which I'll take. Read **_Clean
Code_** (Robert C. Martin, with a foreword by James O. Coplien), which genuinely
changed how I look at my own code; some of its lines live in my head now. I also
went to my first **Google DevFest** (GDG Bandung 2023) — being in a room full of
people who build things is its own kind of fuel. The
[Steam year in review](https://store.steampowered.com/replay/76561198142939511/2023)
has the hours.

## Gear

One purchase to rule them all: a **MacBook Air M2**. I still catch myself just
looking at it. Beyond the vanity, it made moving between coursework, GIGIH, and
side projects genuinely frictionless.

## Carrying into 2024

- Go deeper on data science — this year was the on-ramp, not the destination.
- Keep the DevFest energy: show up where builders are.
- Start the thesis before it starts me.

I ended the year the way I like to — makan-makan with friends on New Year's, in a
city that stopped feeling new. 2023 was a reset, and it took.
